# Daggerfall Forever (DFF)

**Daggerfall Forever** is an open-source, single-player, offline-first fork of **upstream DFO** (itself built on the Daggerfall Unity foundation). Its goal is to deliver a self-contained, modular, and deeply customizable single-player Daggerfall experience - with zero monetization, no persistent MMO servers, and full player control over every system.

> *Your game, your rules. Open source, customizable, without monetization, and expanded with new gameplay systems.*

---

## Core Vision & What DFF Changes

* **Purely Offline Single-Player:** Removes monetization, the Aura Armory, online shop, cloud accounts, and live-service/MMO tails while keeping isolated save and `userData` folders so your DFO saves remain untouched.
* **Online Features Brought Offline (Toggleable):** Play with **Offline Firmament** constellations, **Offline Professions & Gathering**, **Offline Player Housing & Shipyards**, **Unified Empire Banking**, and **24/7 City Infrastructure** on your own schedule.
* **Emergent World & New Gameplay:** Adds **Living Tamriel** (regional crises, road checkpoints, urban skirmishes, naval incidents, roaming adventurers, and reputation consequences), **Necromancy & Lichdom** (from mortal necromancer to Ancient Lich), **Portable Alchemy**, **Item Quality & Shop Rolls**, and **Dynamic Perks**.
* **Native JS/JSON Modding:** Lightweight, zero-compiler mod architecture with Safe-Save isolation and Hot-Rescan, alongside support for DFU `.dfmod` texture packs and loose sound/music replacement packs.
